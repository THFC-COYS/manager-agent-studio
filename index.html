Give your chief of staff a soul

The stack is what the agent does. Soul is who the agent is: its name, its identity, how it carries itself around your team, what it optimizes for, what it will never do, and the voice it speaks in. Set the approval matrix, then name the owner

This is the governance step, and for a manager it is the whole product. Your agent will sit close to your team's trust, so the defaults are conservative: it drafts freely, it summarizes for your eyes only, and anything that sends, nudges a person, or leaves the team waits for your word.

Studio rule: no owner, no agent. A manager's agent without a named accountable manager does not ship. The final package will not generate until the owner is named.
